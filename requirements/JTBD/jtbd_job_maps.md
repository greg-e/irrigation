## 2. Core Functional Job Map

Core Functional Job:
When I am responsible for irrigation service at a property, I want to assess current conditions and document what happened so I can keep the property operating as intended over time.

### 2.1 Define

1. Clarify what successful service looks like for the property at this moment.
2. Determine what outcomes matter most before starting work.

### 2.2 Locate

1. Gather relevant prior context, known conditions, and current constraints.
2. Find the information needed to perform a complete assessment.

### 2.3 Prepare

1. Organize work approach for efficient on-site progress.
2. Set expectations for what will be captured and handed off.

### 2.4 Confirm

1. Confirm readiness to perform assessment without major gaps.
2. Validate that required context is sufficient to begin.

### 2.5 Execute

1. Perform assessment work in the real property context.
2. Capture what was observed and what action was taken.

### 2.6 Monitor

1. Track progress and quality during execution.
2. Watch for exceptions, urgency, and unresolved work.

### 2.7 Modify

1. Adjust approach when real conditions differ from plan.
2. Rebalance work between immediate completion and follow-up pathways.

### 2.8 Conclude

1. Finalize visit outcome, evidence, and unresolved work state.
2. Produce a handoff-ready record for downstream decisions.

## 4. Aggregated Job Maps by Role

### 4.1 Field Execution (Technician)

Define:
1. Clarify visit intent and immediate priorities.

Locate:
1. Retrieve current and prior context needed to execute.

Prepare:
1. Organize work flow for real-world interruptions.

Confirm:
1. Validate readiness and identify missing context early.

Execute:
1. Assess conditions and capture observations efficiently.

Monitor:
1. Watch progress, quality, and unresolved items as work unfolds.

Modify:
1. Adapt to field realities without losing completion integrity.

Conclude:
1. Produce defensible completion with clear next-step state.

### 4.2 Follow-Up and Operations (AM / Branch)

Define:
1. Clarify operational objectives for the current cycle.

Locate:
1. Gather full-state context from recent and prior work.

Prepare:
1. Set priority frames for unresolved and urgent work.

Confirm:
1. Confirm decision readiness before actioning follow-up.

Execute:
1. Triage and act on follow-up pathways.

Monitor:
1. Track unresolved work, urgency, and throughput.

Modify:
1. Rebalance plans as property conditions and priorities change.

Conclude:
1. Close loops with explicit handoff outcomes and accountability.

### 4.3 Governance and Quality

Define:
1. Define stable standards independent of channel/context.

Locate:
1. Locate change history and quality signals.

Prepare:
1. Prepare review and approval controls for consistent use.

Confirm:
1. Confirm content quality and standards adherence.

Execute:
1. Apply governance actions with minimal operational disruption.

Monitor:
1. Monitor drift, exceptions, and traceability quality.

Modify:
1. Adjust standards and controls based on observed risks.

Conclude:
1. Preserve coherent records and rationale for future decisions.
